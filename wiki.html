This page explains every part of your Life Suite: what it does, how you use it, and what Muse does with it. When a new feature ships, it gets logged at the bottom.

How the whole thing works

  • Two ways to use it, same data. Talk to Muse in chat (type or voice) — Muse writes it into the suite. Or tap on the site itself. Everything is editable in both places.
  • The site saves everything. Every Add, Edit, check-off and Delete on this site writes straight to your private database. Nothing here is just a picture of another app.
  • Edit anything: every card and row has an Edit button. Tap it, change the fields, tap Save.
  • Private: only your email gets in, with a one-time code. No password.
  • Tablet: leave it open — pages refresh themselves every minute.

Homepage

Your glance page, with exactly the blocks from your outline: Weeks Left, Today in a nutshell, To Do (check things off right there, or add a task for today), Email that needs action, Life reminders, Event suggestions, Upcoming items of note, Grocery dash, Notifications/insights, and Shipping status. The blocks fill in from Muse's daily work — briefings, inbox cleanups, deliveries.

Tasks (inside Admin)

Your Tweek, rebuilt here. Week view with your frog (🐸) and gold stars first. Check off, filter Personal/Work, jump weeks. Edit any task to change its title, day, Personal/Work, priority, color marker, or notes — and to add subtasks or delete the task. The + Add under each day starts a task on that day. Repeating tasks: tell Muse the task and how often — Muse sets the repeat (nothing repeats without you naming the frequency).

CRM

Your people. All 78 of your Google Contacts are loaded, birthdays included. The queue sorts by who you're most overdue to reach — tap Contacted today when you talk to someone, or Edit a card to change their birthday, your reach-out rhythm (every N days), last-contact date, or the notes (emails, phones, how you met, gift ideas — whatever you want remembered). Add people with the form at the bottom, or tell Muse.

Health & Fitness

The umbrella page links three working tools. Journal: a nightly prompt; write and save entries, edit or delete them later — or dictate to Muse and it files the entry. Meals: your week of meals; tap + Add or Edit on any day, set calories, and each day totals itself against 1,600. Notebook also sits under Health in your outline for the physical side.

Notebook

A real notebook in the suite: write notes, edit them, pin the important ones, split Personal / Work. Muse writes here too when you dictate notes in chat. Next pipe: your Dynalist lists mirrored in.

Admin

The house. Inventory: every supply with how many you have, where it lives, and the reorder point — tap − / + as you use things; Edit changes any field; anything at its reorder point appears at the top and belongs on To Buy. Buying (linked from Admin): To Buy and Grocery lists — check off, edit, delete, add. "I need X" in chat lands on Grocery; "buy X" means Muse stages a real checkout for your approval.

Money

Accounts you can add and edit, with a live net-worth total. Budgets, bills, and automatic categorization switch on when the bank feed connects — then there's no typing at all.

Vanity

Fashion goals and beauty routine — two simple lists, fully editable.

Fun & Recreation

Your "What Should I Do?" picker (46 activities), an editable idea list for things to do, and links to Goals (the bucket list) and Weeks (your life in a grid of weeks — gold is this week).

Events

Structure page for now: your calendar feed and nearby suggestions land here in the next pipe. Your calendar itself already flows through Muse (briefings, invites, conflicts).

Data governance

The control room (your COCKPIT, filed here): live counts from the database, the automations running for you, data sources, and application health.
